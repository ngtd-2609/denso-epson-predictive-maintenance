2. MEASUREMENT SYSTEM
Apparatus: Shinkawa Electric rotor kit AA31-020
Sensor: Seiko Epson M-A342 3-axis digital quartz vibration sensor
Mounting: upper bearing housing
Channels:
- X: lateral direction
- Y: axial direction
- Z: vertical direction

Measured quantity: vibration velocity
Raw unit: mm/s
Nominal sampling frequency: 3000 Hz
Nominal rotation speed: 1200 RPM = 20 Hz fundamental frequency

4. DATA VERSION
Version used: Epson trimmed dataset.

The provider states that the first 30000 samples (10 seconds)
containing startup/transient behavior were removed.

The local pipeline therefore DOES NOT remove another 10 seconds.

Observed file lengths are taken from the actual CSV files and may differ
from approximate values stated on the public webpage.

5. LABEL AND PROVENANCE
Labels are inferred from Epson filename/configuration mapping
and recorded in public_manifest.csv.

recording_id identifies a measurement file.
It is NOT a verified session ID.

session_id remains unknown because the current evidence does not contain
per-file measurement date/session/remount/device-instance metadata.

The paper describes 16 independent data files per class,
but this does not provide enough per-file metadata to prove
session-independent evaluation.

8. KNOWN LIMITATIONS
- No verified per-file session metadata.
- Near-duplicate or time-shifted duplicate analysis is not yet complete.
- Single controlled rig and one nominal speed only.
- No claim of transfer to another machine, sensor, factory or speed.
- CSV files do not contain per-sample timestamps, so timestamp jitter cannot be assessed.
- Dataset-specific redistribution/commercial-use permission has not been verified.
- No detector or generator result is claimed in Person 1 work.

9. USAGE BOUNDARY
Suitable at this stage for file-level research and baseline development
with the limitations above explicitly disclosed.

Do not describe the current evidence as:
- DENSO factory data
- multi-speed validation
- session-independent validation
- prediction of remaining useful life
- proof of deployment/generalization to another machine
